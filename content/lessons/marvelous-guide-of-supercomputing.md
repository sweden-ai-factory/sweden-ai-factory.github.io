The guide is designed to give you a practical introduction to some of the concepts you are most likely to encounter when working with AI and high-performance computing. In about 30 minutes, you will go through the basics of computer systems, supercomputing, machine learning and LLMs. There are also explainations of concepts such as RAG, fine-tuning, AI agents, containers and Kubernetes.
